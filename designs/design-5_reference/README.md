# Design 5: National & Observance Day Vector Art

**Directory:** `designs/design5_national_observance/`

## Visual Archetype & DNA
- Modern layered vector illustration with smooth shading and dignified heroic characters.
- Patriotic national symbolism: Raised National Flag of Bangladesh, Sipahi-Janata solidarity (soldier & citizen united), National Monument (Jatiya Smriti Soudho), water lilies (Shapla), peace doves.
- Stacked block typography with distinct calendar date badge (`07/11 2026`).
- **Strict Rule:** ZERO commercial selling / NO Canva pricing (pure brand goodwill and national pride).
- Ideal for: National holidays, independence celebrations, cultural festivals, memorial days.
