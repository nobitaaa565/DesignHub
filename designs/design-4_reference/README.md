# Design 4: Poetic Conceptual Art & Physical Metaphor

**Directory:** `designs/design4_conceptual_art/`

## Visual Archetype & DNA
- Museum-grade fine-art directional lighting and physical real-world tool (wooden pencil, drafting pen) resting beside a tangible 3D Canva squircle token.
- S-curve transformation: Drawn line morphs into a luminous ribbon of translucent frosted colored glass, climaxing in an origami glass blossom gently guided by an adult hand.
- Tall condensed ivory typography with golden-amber highlighted price pill badge.
- Ideal for: Creative empowerment ('Anyone Can Create'), high-art brand launches, thought leadership.
