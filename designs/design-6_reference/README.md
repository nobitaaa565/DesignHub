# Design 6: Real Model & Floating 3D Feature Badges (Fintech / Pro Subscription)

**Directory:** `designs/design-6_reference/`

## Visual Archetype & DNA
- **Atmosphere & Lighting:** Dark tech radial aperture / futuristic HUD concentric circles with vibrant neon rim illumination (cyan `#00C4CC`, magenta/pink `#E11D48`, and royal violet `#7D2AE8`).
- **Human Connection:** Authentic, charismatic photographic model (smiling Bangladeshi creator/student) looking directly at the viewer with warm, inviting enthusiasm.
- **Hero Device & Interaction:** Hand holding a modern smartphone (e.g. iPhone with Dynamic Island) angled toward the foreground, displaying the live mobile subscription dashboard or creative canvas.
- **Floating 3D Feature Tokens:** Realistic, tangible white 3D squircle app/feature tiles orbiting the phone with soft ambient drop shadows and beveled specular edges (e.g., Canva 3D Logo, Magic Studio Wand, 100M+ Stock Media, Pro Brand Fonts).
- **Offer Container:** High-contrast, glowing geometric badge (magenta/amber neon capsule or chamfered hex-tag) highlighting bold numbers (e.g. `৫০ টাকা` / `৩ মাস` or `৯৯ টাকা`).
- **Header Layout:** Symmetrical dual-logo header (Design Hub brand logo on the top-left, official product/payment badge on the top-right).
- **Ideal For:** Direct-response subscription sales, feature-rich tool showcases, mobile checkout promos, and commercial bKash/Nagad campaign ads.
