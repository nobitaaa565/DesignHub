## Visual Architecture
1. **Background & Atmosphere:**
   - Split minimalist studio backdrop: elegant ivory cream surface on the left, curving deep emerald/forest green architectural arch on the right with soft rim lighting.
2. **Hero Device Mockup (Right Side):**
   - Modern slim laptop (MacBook/Ultrabook) open on desk, displaying the live software/AI interface ("What can I help with?").
3. **Tactile 3D Glass Token (Foreground Center):**
   - A tangible, photorealistic rounded glass squircle app token (emerald green glass with beveled reflections and white brand logo) resting on the desk in front of the laptop.
4. **Headline & Feature Tags (Top-Left):**
   - High-contrast typography: tagline `SMARTER IDEAS EVERYDAY` above bold dark emerald title `ChatGPT / Canva Pro Premium Access`.
   - Sleek pill feature tags: `⚡ GPT-4o / Magic Studio`, `✨ Advanced AI`, `📄 1TB Storage`.
5. **Price Card & CTA (Bottom-Left):**
   - Big dark emerald rounded card with yellow `ONLY` tag, stark-white `৳150 / ৳25` price, and `PER MONTH` subtitle.
   - Rounded pill CTA button: `Order Now >`.
